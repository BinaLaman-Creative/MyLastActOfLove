To Syafisah Nur'ain,

Jujur, orang mau minta maaf sangat-sangat dekat dia for everything that has happened. Orang tahu mungkin words alone won’t be enough to explain how sorry orang sebenarnya, but please know that orang genuinely regret everything. Orang minta maaf kalau sepanjang kita kenal, orang pernah buat dia sakit hati, kecewa, menangis or even made her question whether she was ever treated right. Orang minta maaf kalau layanan orang tidak cukup baik, kalau effort orang nda macam yang dia harapkan and kalau ada certain moments yang buat dia rasa macam orang nda appreciate dia enough. It was never my intention to make you feel that way, but orang faham that intentions don’t erase the pain caused by actions.

To be honest, orang sendiri kecewa dengan diri orang. Orang keep thinking about everything that happened, about the things orang could have done differently and about the decisions that orang made without properly thinking about the consequences. Salah orang sebab terlalu cepat buat keputusan when sebenarnya orang sendiri belum sembuh sepenuhnya. Orang thought that I was ready to move forward, thought that I could handle everything like I used to, but deep down, there were still so many things that I hadn’t healed from. And unfortunately, you had to experience the side of me that was still struggling with myself.

Orang nda mau jadikan my past as an excuse for the way I treated you. Whatever happened, orang tetap kena bertanggungjawab atas tindakan orang sendiri. Orang sedar that you didn’t deserve to be caught in the middle of my unfinished healing process. You deserves someone who can give you genuine happiness, consistent effort and a peace of mind. And I’m truly sorry if orang failed to give you those things.

Orang msu dia tahu yang orang bukan sekadar minta maaf because things didn’t turn out the way orang expected. Orang menyesal sebab orang sedar there were moments when orang could have been more understanding, more patient and more careful with your feelings. Kalau boleh undur masa, there are so many things orang would do differently. But I know I can’t change what has already happened. All I can do now is acknowledge my mistakes, learn from them and make sure I don’t repeat the same things again.

Orang sedang cuba untuk become myself again, slowly fixing the things that have been broken inside me. Orang mau belajar how to handle my emotions better, how to communicate properly and how to stop letting my past affect the people who come into my life. It won’t happen overnight, but orang genuinely want to change. Not just through words, but through actions. Orang nda mau keep saying sorry without actually becoming a better person. And please, don’t ever think that orang nda realise the mistakes orang sendiri. Orang sedar, orang fikir, and orang menyesal lebih daripada apa yang orang mampu explain through this text. Orang mungkin nda pandai tunjuk everything that orang rasa, but that doesn’t mean orang tidak care about how you feels. Orang cuma berharap dia tahu that this apology comes from a sincere place, not because orang terpaksa or because orang mau everything to go back to how it used to be.

Once again, I’m truly sorry for everything. For every disappointment, every hurtful moment and every time orang made dia feel less than what you deserves. Orang nda expect dia untuk terus forget everything or accept this apology immediately. Take all the time you needs. Orang just hope that one day, you can understand that orang genuinely regret what happened.

I’m sorry for not being the best version of myself when I should have been more careful with someone’s feelings. I can’t promise that my past never happened, but I can promise myself that I’ll learn from it and never let the same mistakes define the person I become.

Hanif Hisyam.
